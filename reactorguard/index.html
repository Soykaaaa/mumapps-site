Reactor Guard: Tower Defense

Carry energy, stop robot waves. For iPhone and iPad.

Robots are marching on your reactor. Charge up at the core, carry the energy to your towers and keep them firing. A full tower fires 50% faster; an empty one stops. Build, power and upgrade your defences, then hold the line against wave after wave of machines and giant Titan bosses.
