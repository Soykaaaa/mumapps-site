Reactor Guard – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Reactor Guard is a game made by MUM Apps, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game uses, why it uses it, and what choices you have. If you have any questions, write to us at info@mumapps.com.

In short

  • Ads by Google AdMob: optional ads you can watch for a reward, and from level 10 an occasional short ad between levels. Any purchase, or Remove Ads, turns the ads between levels off.
  • No tracking: the game does not ask for permission to track you, so it never gets your device's advertising identifier.
  • No account: the game never asks for your name, e-mail address, or password.
  • The online leaderboard and cloud save use an anonymous player ID and a random player name.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The game is fully playable offline.

1. Information kept on your device

  • Your game progress and settings (such as levels, stars, chips, upgrades, and sound settings).
  • A record of completed purchases, so that no purchase is delivered twice.
  • A sign-in token for the online features and your anonymous player ID, stored in the iOS Keychain. If iCloud Keychain is turned on, Apple syncs them to your other devices. This lets you keep the same online profile after reinstalling the game or moving to a new device.

2. Online features (Unity Gaming Services)

When your device is online, the game uses Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies, for two features:

  • Leaderboard: your Guardian Points total, your rank, and a random player name (for example, "BraveOtter"), which other players can see. You cannot choose or type in a name, so no personal name is ever shown.
  • Cloud save: a copy of your saved game, so that your progress can be restored if you reinstall the game or move to a new device.

To provide these features, Unity processes an anonymous player ID that is created when the game first connects, the score and the saved-game copy described above, and the technical information needed to run the services: your IP address (which Unity may use to estimate your approximate region and for security), basic device and app information, and diagnostic and crash data from Unity's software. We use this information only to provide these features; Unity may also use basic usage data to operate and improve its services. None of it is used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Unity may process this information in the United States and other countries. For details, see Unity's privacy policy.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are made through the Apple App Store, and Apple processes the payment. The game receives only a confirmation of which product was bought, along with a transaction identifier, so that the item can be added to your game. The Unity software the game uses to connect to the App Store also sends Unity information about the shop and your purchases: which product was bought, the transaction identifier, an identifier for this app on your device (the identifier for vendor, not the advertising identifier), and basic diagnostic data. Unity uses this information to operate and improve its purchasing services. For details, see Apple's privacy policy and Unity's privacy policy.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Some rewards can be earned by watching a short video ad: double chips after a won level, a second chance when the core falls, and a free daily gift. These ads only play when you tap one of these buttons, and each reward has a daily limit.

From level 10 on, a short ad may also play when you leave a finished level, at most about once every four minutes and never during a level. These ads between levels are never shown to players who have made any in-app purchase, and the Remove Ads purchase in the Shop turns them off for good.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC, and Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland). To request, show, and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the game collects: your IP address (from which Google estimates your approximate location), device and app information (such as the device model, operating system and language, and the app version), information about the ads shown and your interactions with them, and diagnostic data. We do not ask for permission to track you, so the game and Google never receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and the ads are not based on your activity in other companies' apps or websites. Ads are limited to content suitable for ages 13 and up.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are requested, and you can change it at any time in the game's Settings under Privacy Choices. For details on how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads during a level, and we do not use tracking SDKs.
  • We do not read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not ask for permission to track you.
  • We do not access your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

6. Children

Reactor Guard is suitable for a general audience and does not ask anyone for personal information. Apart from the technical information described in sections 2 and 4, the only data kept online is the anonymous player ID, the random name, the score, and the saved-game copy. If you are a parent and would like this data deleted, use Delete Online Data in the game's Settings, or contact us as described in section 7.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your online profile, leaderboard entry, and cloud save are kept until you delete them. You can delete them yourself at any time: open Settings in the game and tap Delete Online Data. This immediately and permanently deletes your online profile and its cloud save, and removes the sign-in token from the device and from your iOS Keychain. Your progress and purchases on the device stay, and the online features stay off on that device until you tap Go Online, which starts a new online profile with a new random name.

Your leaderboard score cannot be removed from inside the game. After an in-game deletion, it remains only as a score under the random name, with no profile behind it. To have it removed too, or if you cannot use the in-game option, e-mail info@mumapps.com with your online name, which the game shows at the top of the Settings screen ("Your online name") while you are online, so make a note of it before you delete. We will delete the data within 30 days.

Deleting the game removes the progress stored on your device, but not your online data or the sign-in token in your iOS Keychain. To remove your online data, use Delete Online Data before you delete the game, or ask us as described above. Once your online profile is deleted, the token no longer works, and if you reinstall the game, it discards the old token and starts a new profile.

If you use Reset Progress in the game's Settings, the game starts over with a new online profile and a new random name. The previous profile's leaderboard entry and cloud save are kept. If you want them deleted, send us the old online name, so make a note of it before you reset.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU or the Turkish data protection law, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, write to info@mumapps.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
