Reactor Guard – Support

Contact us: info@mumapps.com
We usually reply within two business days. Please include your device model (for example, iPhone 16 or iPad Air), your iOS or iPadOS version, and, if your question is about the leaderboard or your progress, your online name (shown at the top of the Settings screen while you are online).

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop while you are online and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom left. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to make the purchases. This restores one-time purchases (Premium and the Starter Pack). Chips and boosters you have bought are saved with your progress, so they come back with your cloud save.

I reinstalled the game or got a new device. Where is my progress?

Whenever you are online, the game keeps a copy of your progress in the cloud. Open the game with an internet connection, and your progress comes back automatically with the message "Welcome back! Your progress was restored from the cloud." On a new iPhone or iPad, sign in with the same Apple ID and make sure iCloud Keychain is enabled on both devices. If your progress does not come back, write to us and include your old online name if you know it.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. The whole game can be played offline; only purchases need a connection to the App Store. The leaderboard and your cloud save update the next time you are online.

What are Guardian Points, and how does the leaderboard work?

Each level you win earns you a score based on your stars, the core health you have left, how quickly you finish, and how much scrap you leave unspent. Later levels are worth more. Your Guardian Points are the sum of your best scores on all levels, so replaying a level never lowers them. To open the leaderboard, tap the podium button at the top right of the home screen.

Can I change my leaderboard name?

Not at the moment. Every player is given a random name, so no real names are ever shown.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the game and tap Delete Online Data. It permanently deletes your online profile and cloud save right away; your progress and purchases on the device stay. Your leaderboard score stays as an entry with no profile behind it. To have it removed too, note your online name (Settings → "Your online name") before you delete, then send it to info@mumapps.com, and we will remove it within 30 days. For details, see the privacy policy.

I found a bug.

Thank you! Please write to us describing what happened, and include the level number and your device model. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
